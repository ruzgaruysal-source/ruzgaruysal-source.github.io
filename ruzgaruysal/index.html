Who is Rüzgar Uysal?

Rüzgar Uysal is the Founder and CEO of BeVibome, an online brand that sells problem-solving home products to customers across the United States.

Born in İstanbul, he is part of the joint program of Dokuz Eylül University in İzmir and the University at Albany, SUNY, in New York.
